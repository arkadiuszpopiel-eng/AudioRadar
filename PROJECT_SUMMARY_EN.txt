SOLUTION - NOISE REDUCTION:
RecordingController._apply_noise_reduction() with scipy.signal:
- Butterworth high-pass filter (80Hz cutoff) removes rumble/hum
- Noise gate (0.01 RMS threshold) attenuates background noise by 50%
- Soft clipping (-0.95 to 0.95) prevents distortion
- Enabled by default (noise_reduction_enabled=True)
